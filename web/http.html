An HTTP/1.1 request parser, proved

The parser of examples/http, proved free of out-of-bounds reads and overflow for every input, compiled to WebAssembly by bastioc1 --arch wasm32. Edit the request: the page shows what the module makes of it. The page sends each line with CR LF; a folded line, a space before a colon or a request that names its body twice is rejected.
